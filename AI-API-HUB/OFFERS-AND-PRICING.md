# Ready-to-Sell Offers & Pricing

These are starting commercial ranges, not fixed quotes. Final pricing depends on scope, integrations, production requirements, and support.

## Fastest-entry offers

### 1. Automation Repair Sprint
**Buyer:** companies/agencies with a broken n8n/API workflow.
**Pilot:** diagnose one workflow, identify root cause, fix and test it.
**Starting price:** $75–$250.
**Delivery target:** 1–2 days.
**Upsell:** monitoring, documentation, additional workflows, monthly maintenance.

### 2. AI/API Integration Pilot
**Buyer:** SaaS companies, agencies, e-commerce businesses.
**Pilot:** connect one API/model to one existing business process.
**Starting price:** $150–$500.
**Delivery target:** 2–5 days.
**Upsell:** production hardening and additional integrations.

### 3. Lead Automation Pilot
**Buyer:** agencies, B2B companies, service businesses.
**Pilot:** capture leads → enrich/classify → CRM → automated follow-up/notification.
**Starting price:** $250–$750.
**Delivery target:** 3–7 days.
**Upsell:** full sales pipeline and monthly optimization.

### 4. Customer Support AI Pilot
**Buyer:** e-commerce, SaaS, agencies, service businesses.
**Pilot:** knowledge base + AI response layer + human escalation.
**Starting price:** $300–$1,000.
**Delivery target:** 3–7 days.
**Upsell:** WhatsApp, CRM, analytics, multilingual support.

### 5. Document Automation Pilot
**Buyer:** finance, logistics, insurance, real estate, operations teams.
**Pilot:** PDF/email/document → OCR/extraction → structured data → destination system.
**Starting price:** $250–$900.
**Delivery target:** 3–7 days.
**Upsell:** batch processing, validation, dashboards, integrations.

## Higher-value offers

### 6. AI Agent Implementation
**Starting price:** $750–$3,000+.
**Scope:** one production-oriented agent with tools, guardrails, logging, and human approval where needed.

### 7. CRM + WhatsApp Automation
**Starting price:** $500–$2,500+.
**Scope:** lead intake, qualification, routing, follow-up, CRM updates, notifications.

### 8. E-commerce Automation
**Starting price:** $500–$3,000+.
**Scope:** Shopify/WooCommerce + CRM/support/inventory/marketing integrations.

### 9. AI/Automation Technical Audit
**Starting price:** $100–$500.
**Deliverable:** current-state review, automation opportunities, architecture, recommended tools, priorities, and implementation estimate.

### 10. Monthly Automation Maintenance
**Starting price:** $300–$1,500+/month.
**Scope:** monitoring, fixes, small improvements, workflow maintenance, API changes, and support.

## Pricing rules

- Quote fixed scope for pilots.
- Ask for payment before starting small fixed-price work when appropriate.
- Never promise unlimited revisions.
- Define integrations, inputs, outputs, acceptance criteria, and support window.
- Credentials stay outside GitHub source code.
- Use the smallest pilot that proves business value.

## Sales positioning

Do not lead with a list of APIs. Lead with the operational problem and measurable outcome.

Examples:
- “We can automate your lead qualification and CRM updates.”
- “We can repair and harden your existing n8n workflow.”
- “We can turn incoming PDFs into validated structured records automatically.”
- “We can connect your AI assistant to the systems your team already uses.”

## Preferred sales ladder

1. Free/short discovery conversation when useful.
2. Paid diagnostic or small pilot.
3. Production implementation.
4. Monthly maintenance/optimization.
5. Expand into adjacent workflows.
